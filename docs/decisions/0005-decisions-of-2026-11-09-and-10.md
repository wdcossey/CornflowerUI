# ADR 0005 — Decisions of 2026-11-09 and 2026-11-10: the tilt and the motion

**Date:** 2026-11-10 · **Status:** Accepted

## Context

The last Tier 0 items were the tilt ([#22](https://github.com/wdcossey/CornflowerUI/issues/22)) and the motion
recordings ([#24](https://github.com/wdcossey/CornflowerUI/issues/24)). On the evening of 2026-11-09 the
maintainer answered how both should be measured and built, on the working site. The work was done on
2026-11-10: [#60](https://github.com/wdcossey/CornflowerUI/pull/60) and
[#62](https://github.com/wdcossey/CornflowerUI/pull/62) (the tilt grid and its review),
[#65](https://github.com/wdcossey/CornflowerUI/pull/65) (the tilt behaviour) and
[#66](https://github.com/wdcossey/CornflowerUI/pull/66) (the motion). This records the answers and what the
measurements settled. The decision on larger screens, made the same evening, is
[ADR 0004](0004-larger-screens-as-an-extension-pack.md).

## Decision

### How to measure and build (2026-11-09)

| Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| How densely to measure the tilt (`D-tilt-measure`) | **A dense grid**: four Button sizes, 25 press points each, every frame logged (`run.ps1 -Probe tiltgrid`) | The first readings (#58) had a few points on one size. A model fitted to 100 presses can be tested at any of them |
| How faithful the tilt's timing is (`D-tilt-fidelity`) | **Frame by frame**: a frame loop on `CornflowerClock`, tested at chosen milliseconds against the readings, not Avalonia `Transitions` | `Transitions` run on Avalonia's internal clock and can only be tested at their end value ([ADR 0003](0003-decisions-on-the-tier-0-infrastructure.md)); the tilt is felt under the finger, frame by frame |
| How to measure motion (`D-motion-approach`) | **Per-frame readings in the app**, as the tilt probe does; video only for what the app can't read, the page transitions | Readings are exact and need no frame stepping |
| The ToggleSwitch on the 720p profile (`Q-toggleswitch-720p`) | **Run it now**, in #24 | To learn whether #58's tap limits scale with the screen |
| A Windows 10 Mobile capture pass (`Q-wp10-capture`) | **At Tier 4**, with the question of borrowing 10 Mobile's design for controls 8.1 never had (`Q-borrow-wp10`; the maintainer leans towards borrowing) | Nothing before Tier 4 depends on it |
| The tilt's screen-position bias (`D-tilt-bias`) | **Try to reproduce it**, in a time box; if that fails, record a deviation under 1° | It was under 1°, but grew with the control's distance from the screen's centre, so it looked like geometry, not noise |
| What Tier 1 may start early (`D-tier1-start`) | **The type ramp, #26**, once the four Tier 0 PRs are open and green; nothing else from Tier 1 until they are merged | #26 names the resources the extension pack overrides (ADR 0004) |

### The tilt

**The 8.1 tilt is the Silverlight toolkit's straight rule applied, each frame, to the press point as the tilted
element is drawn.** The angle on each axis is 17.19° (0.3 rad) × the press point's offset from the centre, as a
fraction of the size. Depth is −25 × (1 − (|rx| + |ry|) / 17.19°). The eye sits 0.8 of the way from the control's
centre to the screen's centre (the 0.8 translation the phone applies and cancels), at 1,055 view px on the
reference profile, which is fitted. Taking the press point through the drawn tilt explains both the 0.08 |d| term
#60 fitted and the bias it couldn't model. Over the 100 grid presses the angles are out by 0.019° at worst (rms
0.006°), and a run not in the fit agrees within 0.017°. **So there is no deviation to record.** For comparison,
#60's curve was out by 0.38° rms raw, 1.26° at worst (#62).

| Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The transform | **One perspective `MatrixTransform` on the template's root**, set at animation priority | `Rotate3DTransform` has no depth offset (`GlobalOffsetZ`) and puts its eye over its own origin. A test holds that the matrix equals it when there is no depth and the eye is centred. Animation priority leaves the root's own transform in place |
| Timing | A press jumps to the held values; a move follows the finger; a release, a lost capture or the finger leaving the control holds 180 ms, then eases back with a sine over 195 ms | #60's 1,199 release frames: a sine ease-out fits within 0.03 of the full value |
| What wasn't measured | A finger coming back onto the control tilts it again, and a lost capture counts as a release, as in the toolkit | Chosen, not measured; the PR says so |
| Open | Whether the eye distance scales with the screen (a 720p grid run, `20261110-0851-dev10`, is in hand), and a press within about 3 px of an edge: on the phone it clicks (all 36 taps of `20261110-0822-dev11`), where the drawn tilt moves our control from under the finger. #65 matches the phone or records the difference | |

### The motion

What the readings showed changes how motion is measured:

- **Theme transitions are invisible to the app.** Popups, page transitions and the ToggleSwitch knob's
  reposition run in the compositor, and leave nothing a per-frame reading sees. They are measured from video:
  `run.ps1 -Record` records the emulator's display with `ffmpeg gdigrab` from the interactive session, with each
  frame's time. Template storyboards (the MenuFlyout's, the ProgressRing's) are read every frame.
- **The emulator animates on a 64 Hz clock** (15.625 ms steps, the same every run), so many runs together give a
  curve finer than one recording's 25 ms.

| Motion | Measured | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ToggleSwitch knob, after release | `KeySpline` 0.1,0.9 0.2,1 over 370 ms, within 0.001 of the travel | #66 |
| ToggleSwitch tap limits on the 720p profile | The same in view px as on the reference profile (moved under about 18, held under about 1,020 ms), so not raw px | `20261110-0733-dev10` |
| PlaneProjection's camera | 1000 view px from the screen, looking at the element's centre | `projections` probe |
| Page turnstile (default and `CommonNavigationTransitionInfo`) | Only the incoming page moves, about an axis 44 px left of it and 99 px behind the screen. Back: `ExponentialEase` (6) from +42° over 430 ms; forward: from −67° (the first frame seen) over 391 ms | `pages` probe, recorded |
| Page slide | `ExponentialEase` (6) from 200 px over 350 ms in; an accelerating slide out | the same |
| Continuum | Fade, black, scale 0.94 to 1 | the same |
| Popups | Timings to ±25 ms, not curves (README "Motion"). The MenuFlyout's storyboard: `ScaleX` linear 380 ms, then `ScaleY` linear 120 ms | `motion` probe |
| ProgressRing `Active` | Five dots at the default size, six at 120 px, 150 to 167 ms apart, a 4.13 s cycle by the app's clock, 4% shorter than the template's keyframes give | `ring` probe |

## Consequences

- A control's plan takes its motion from `tools/refapp/README.md`, "Motion". A theme transition is still tested
  at its end value (ADR 0003), but its easing and duration in the theme are the measured ones, named in the plan.
- A motion that must match frame by frame, like the tilt, is a frame loop on `CornflowerClock`.
- Not yet measured: the pickers' and the full Flyout's per-row swivel curves, and the turnstile's forward start
  angle. The controls that need them (Tier 3) measure them first.
- The ProgressRing theme ([#28](https://github.com/wdcossey/CornflowerUI/issues/28)) compares its cycle with
  both 4.13 s and the template's keyframes, and says which it follows.
