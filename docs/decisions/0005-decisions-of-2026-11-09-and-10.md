# ADR 0005 — Decisions of 2026-11-09 and 2026-11-10: the tilt and the motion

**Date:** 2026-11-10 · **Status:** Proposed in [#67](https://github.com/wdcossey/CornflowerUI/pull/67). The first
table is the maintainer's answers of 2026-11-09. The tilt and motion sections record what the work measured, and

## Decision

### How to measure and build (2026-11-09)

| Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| How densely to measure the tilt (`D-tilt-measure`) | **A dense grid**: four Button sizes, 25 press points each, every frame logged (`run.ps1 -Probe tiltgrid`) | The first readings (#58) had a few points on one size. A model fitted to 100 presses can be tested at any of them |
| How faithful the tilt's timing is (`D-tilt-fidelity`) | **Frame by frame**: a frame loop on `CornflowerClock`, tested at chosen milliseconds against the readings, not Avalonia `Transitions` | `Transitions` run on Avalonia's internal clock and can only be tested at their end value ([ADR 0003](0003-decisions-on-the-tier-0-infrastructure.md)); the tilt is felt under the finger, frame by frame |
| How to measure motion (`D-motion-approach`) | **Per-frame readings in the app**, as the tilt probe does; video only for what the app can't read, the page transitions | Readings are exact and need no frame stepping |
| The ToggleSwitch on the 720p profile (`Q-toggleswitch-720p`) | **Run it now**, in #24 | To learn whether #58's tap limits scale with the screen |
| A Windows 10 Mobile capture pass (`Q-wp10-capture`) | **At Tier 4**, with the question of borrowing 10 Mobile's design for controls 8.1 never had (`Q-borrow-wp10`; the maintainer leans towards borrowing) | Nothing before Tier 4 depends on it |
| The tilt's screen-position bias (`D-tilt-bias`) | **Try to reproduce it**, in a time box; if that fails, record a deviation under 1° | It was under 1°, but grew with the control's distance from the screen's centre, so it looked like geometry, not noise |
| What Tier 1 may start early (`D-tier1-start`) | **The type ramp, #26**, once the rest of Tier 0 is open and green (on 2026-11-10, #20, #21, #22 and #24); nothing else from Tier 1 until Tier 0 is merged | #26 names the resources the extension pack overrides (ADR 0004) |

### The tilt

**The 8.1 tilt is the Silverlight toolkit's straight rule applied, each frame, to the press point as the tilted
element is drawn.** The angle on each axis is 17.19° (0.3 rad) × the press point's offset from the centre, as a
fraction of the size. Depth is −25 × (1 − (|rx| + |ry|) / 17.19°). The press point is taken through a perspective
whose eye is PlaneProjection's camera, 999 view px from the screen as #66 measured it, shifted 0.76 of the way
from the control's centre towards the screen's centre. The shift is the one fitted number. Taking the press point
through the drawn tilt explains both the 0.08 |d| term #60 fitted and the bias it couldn't model. Over the 100
grid presses the angles are out by 0.026° at worst. **So the bias needs no deviation.** For comparison, #60's
curve was out by 0.38° rms raw, 1.26° at worst (#62).

| Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The transform | **One perspective `MatrixTransform`**, set at animation priority on the part a template marks with `Tilt.IsTarget`, inside an untilted root | `Rotate3DTransform` has no depth offset (`GlobalOffsetZ`) and puts its eye over its own origin. A test holds that the matrix equals it when there is no depth and the eye is centred. At animation priority the tilt goes over the part's own `RenderTransform` while it tilts and gives it back at rest. *Amended 2026-11-11, after #65's review:* it is drawn after the part's own transform, composed with it, as the phone draws a `PlaneProjection` after a `RenderTransform`, where it first replaced it. That transform changing in place is drawn at once, and the press steps again; a new value for it set while a press has settled shows at the next move or the return, as Avalonia 12.1.4 raises no change for a value set under an animation-priority one. Only a local or styled value is composed: one at animation priority (an `Animation`'s or a `Transitions`' value) can't be read under the tilt's, and the later of the two shows, so a template animates its tilted part's transform on another element |
| The eye | **The measured camera, 999 view px, with only the shift fitted (0.76)** | The grid fixes only the ratio of the eye's offset to its distance. The direct measurement fixes the distance. With 999 and the phone's own 0.8 translation the worst is 0.064°, so the shift is 0.76, not 0.8. Why it differs is open; that the camera looks at the element's centre is assumed |
| Does it scale with the screen? | **No: the same eye in view px fits the 720p profile** (`20261110-0851-dev10`), worst 0.021°. Scaled by raw pixels it would be out by 0.30°, by width 0.11° | The 720p grid is tested too |
| Timing | A press jumps to the held values; a move follows the finger; a release, a lost capture or the finger leaving the control holds 180 ms, then eases back with a sine over 195 ms | #60's 1,199 release frames: a sine ease-out fits them with an rms of 0.03 of the full value (an average, not a bound on each frame) |
| A press at the very edge | **It clicks**, as on the phone: all 36 taps at 0.84, 1.7 and 3.35 view px inside each edge clicked (`20261110-0822-dev11`). Avalonia hit-tests the last composed frame through a `RenderTransform`, so a tilted root moves the control from under the finger. So a template marks the part to tilt with `Tilt.IsTarget`, and its root stays untilted, with a `Transparent` background, to take every hit | Rule 4: the reference decides. A stock template can't be given that layer from outside, so Simple's Button still misses those taps; every Wp81 template that tilts (#27 on) marks its part |
| What wasn't measured | A finger coming back onto the control tilts it again, and a lost capture counts as a release, as in the toolkit. Nested tilt-enabled controls: only the nearest tilts | Chosen, not measured; the PR says so |

### The motion

What the readings showed changes how motion is measured:

- **Theme transitions are invisible to the app.** Popups, page transitions and the ToggleSwitch knob's
  reposition run in the compositor, and leave nothing a per-frame reading sees. They are measured from video:
  `run.ps1 -Record` records the emulator's display with `ffmpeg gdigrab` from the interactive session, with each
  frame's time. Template storyboards (the MenuFlyout's, the ProgressRing's) are read every frame.
- **The emulator animates on a 64 Hz clock** (15.625 ms steps, the same every run), so many runs together give a
  curve finer than one recording's 25 ms.

| Motion | Measured | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ToggleSwitch knob, after release | `KeySpline` 0.1,0.9 0.2,1 over 370 ms, within 0.001 of the travel | #66 |
| ToggleSwitch tap limits on the 720p profile | The distance is the same in view px as on the reference profile (moved 17.76 taps, 18.85 doesn't; 17.47 and 18.33 on the reference), so not raw px. The hold: 984 ms taps and 1,018 doesn't on 720p, against a scatter round 1,020 ms on the reference, so not shown to differ, nor to be the same | `20261110-0733-dev10` |
| PlaneProjection's camera | 999 view px from the screen, looking at the element's centre (tools/refapp/README.md, "Motion", is the source; the tilt uses it) | `projections` probe |
| Page turnstile (default and `CommonNavigationTransitionInfo`) | Only the incoming page moves, about an axis 44 px left of it and 99 px behind the screen. Back: `ExponentialEase` (6) from +42° over 430 ms; forward: an exponential ease-out (5.5) from −67° (the first frame seen) over 391 ms. Exponent 6 fits forward only with a start far off the screen | `pages` probe, recorded |
| Page slide | `ExponentialEase` (6) from 200 px over 350 ms in; an accelerating slide out | the same |
| Continuum | Fade, black, scale 0.94 to 1 | the same |
| Popups | Timings to ±25 ms, not curves (README "Motion"). The MenuFlyout's storyboard: `ScaleX` linear 380 ms, then `ScaleY` linear 120 ms | `motion` probe |
| ProgressRing `Active` | Five dots at the default size, six at 120 px, 150 to 167 ms apart, a 4.13 s cycle by the app's clock, 4% shorter than the template's keyframes give | `ring` probe |

## Consequences

- A control's plan takes its motion from `tools/refapp/README.md`, "Motion". A theme transition is still tested
  at its end value (ADR 0003), but its easing and duration in the theme are the measured ones, named in the plan.
- A motion that must match frame by frame, like the tilt, is a frame loop on `CornflowerClock`.
- Not yet measured: the pickers' and the full Flyout's per-row swivel curves, and the turnstile's forward start
  angle. The controls that need them (Tier 3) measure them first.
- The ProgressRing theme ([#28](https://github.com/wdcossey/CornflowerUI/issues/28)) compares its cycle with
  both 4.13 s and the template's keyframes, and says which it follows.
